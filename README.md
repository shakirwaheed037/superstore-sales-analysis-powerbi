# Superstore Sales Analysis & Executive Dashboard

An interactive **Sales Analytics and Executive Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze Superstore sales performance, identify trends, and provide a clear business overview through interactive visualizations and filters.

## 📊 Project Overview

This project focuses on transforming Superstore sales data into meaningful business insights using **Power BI**.

The project covers the complete analytics workflow, including data preparation, data analysis, DAX calculations, visualization, and dashboard design.

The final outcome is an **Executive Sales Dashboard** that allows users to monitor key sales KPIs and explore sales performance by month, category, sub-category, customer segment, and region.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

* Analyze overall sales performance.
* Monitor important business KPIs.
* Identify monthly sales trends.
* Analyze sales performance across product categories.
* Compare sales across different sub-categories.
* Analyze customer segments.
* Analyze regional sales performance.
* Build an interactive and executive-level Power BI dashboard.
* Present business information in a clear and visually understandable format.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

The Executive Dashboard contains the following visualizations:

### Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard includes KPI cards for:

* **Total Orders**
* **Total Revenue**
* **Total Sales**

These KPIs provide a quick overview of overall business performance.

### Monthly Sales Trend

A line chart is used to visualize sales performance over time.

This helps identify:

* Monthly sales patterns
* Growth or decline trends
* High and low sales periods
* Overall sales movement

### Sales by Category

A donut chart presents total sales distribution across product categories.

This helps compare the contribution of different categories to overall sales.

### Sales by Sub-Category

A TreeMap is used to analyze sales performance across product sub-categories.

This provides a quick visual comparison of the relative contribution of each sub-category.

### Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes interactive slicers for:

* **Customer Segment**
* **Region**

These filters allow users to explore the dashboard according to specific customer groups and geographical regions.
